Cube World · Ocean in Motion

Clare Hooper · Intro to Computer Graphics · Project 2

A seascape made only from cubes, drawn with plain WebGL (no libraries). Waves roll in on Bézier curves and clouds drift across the sky on Bézier paths. Note: the highest resolutions need a fairly powerful computer.
